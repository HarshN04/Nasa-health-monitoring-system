Values from real astronauts (SpaceX Inspiration4, NASA Open Science Data Repository OSD-569 & OSD-575), placed on our mission timeline.

What should I do?

    How to read this page. Shaded band = your normal (your first 14 mission days, ±2 SD). Watch = outside it 3+ days in a row. Act now = very far off today or past a safety limit. Radiation only adds up, so it is shown as the mission total against the 600 mSv limit. Blood tests use the lab's normal range, or your own pre-flight level when the lab gives none.
